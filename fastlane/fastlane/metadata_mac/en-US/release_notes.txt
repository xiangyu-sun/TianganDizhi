- More accurate solar terms: terms that begin in the afternoon used to appear a day late and now appear on the day they begin
- The "Use UTC+8 for festivals and solar terms" setting now also covers solar terms and today's festival, so users outside China can follow their local date or China's date
- The calendar now uses the traditional names 冬月 and 臘月 for the 11th and 12th lunar months, and 閏 for leap months
- The Bazi Da Yun starting age follows the corrected solar-term dates and may be one year earlier for some birth dates
- Due to Apple's new rules, apps can no longer bundle the Weibei (魏碑) font, so Home Screen and desktop widgets now use the open-source LXGW WenKai (霞鶩文楷) font instead. The app itself still uses the system's Weibei font, and Lock Screen widgets use the system font
- Bug fixes and stability improvements
