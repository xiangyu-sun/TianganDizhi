- New: TianganDizhi now speaks English and Simplified Chinese. The app, widgets and menu bar follow your Mac's language, and Traditional Chinese stays exactly as before
- In English, every term keeps its Chinese character with pinyin and an English name (子 · Zǐ · Rat), while the clock dial, the trigram wheels and the Ode to the Twelve Hours stay in Chinese
- In Simplified Chinese, terms, festivals and the calendar are shown in Simplified characters with the Weibei SC calligraphy font
- Choose the app's language anytime in the app under Settings › Language. If you already use the app, it stays in Traditional Chinese after the update
- The clock and dials stay usable in small windows
- Bug fixes and stability improvements
